# Fraction Explorer — teacher guide

**Status:** Demo; teacher review required.

**Goal:** Students use shaded equal-length partitions to interpret numerator and denominator.

**Prompt:** What happens to the size of each part as the denominator increases? How can 2/4 and 1/2 both represent one-half?

**Misconceptions:** More denominator parts do not necessarily mean more quantity; partitions must be equal in size.

**Activity:** Ask learners to construct 1/2 in at least three ways; have them explain equivalence aloud.

**Limitation:** Slider allows 2–12 equal sections; it does not directly compare two fraction bars side by side. No standards have been verified.

**Approval:** Pending teacher reviewer, date, and decision.
