# The Water Cycle — teacher guide

**Status:** Demo; teacher review required.

**Goal:** Students describe evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection/movement as processes in a connected cycle.

**Prompt:** Must water travel through every step in the pictured order? Where can water remain for a long time?

**Misconceptions:** The water cycle is not a rigid four-step conveyor belt. Clouds contain tiny droplets and/or ice crystals rather than being solely invisible water vapor.

**Activity:** Students trace several hypothetical pathways for a raindrop.

**Limitation:** No proportional quantities or residence times represented. No standards have been verified.

**Approval:** Pending teacher reviewer, date, and decision.
