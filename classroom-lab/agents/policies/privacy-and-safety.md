# Privacy and learner safety

- Never require learner names, email, school or student IDs, photographs, or accounts.
- Never store submissions or assessment histories unless the teacher or instructor explicitly chooses an approved school or institution system and privacy review.
- Do not embed analytics/tracking scripts or public LLM API keys.
- Verify suggested hands-on experiments for the audience's age, setting, materials, and safety. Sections for minors (such as Grade School) need extra care; see their section guide.
- The section's reviewer (the teacher, instructor, or subject expert named in its guide) retains editorial and publication authority.
