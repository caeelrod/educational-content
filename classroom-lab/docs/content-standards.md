# Content quality rubric

An agent should validate each artifact against these criteria before requesting review:

1. **Instructional objective:** One observable, grade-appropriate outcome.
2. **Conceptual accuracy:** Sources are authoritative; uncertainty and model limitations are explicit.
3. **Misconceptions:** At least two likely misconceptions identified and addressed.
4. **Active learning:** A student makes predictions, manipulates variables, explains an outcome, or solves a challenge.
5. **Feedback:** More than an arbitrary success/failure indicator; explanations help understanding.
6. **Accessibility:** Keyboard, touch, high contrast, accessible names, simple language, reduced motion when applicable.
7. **Teacher utility:** Facilitation notes, duration, differentiated prompts, answer guidance.
8. **Safety:** No dangerous experiment instructions or collection of student information.
9. **Standards:** Alignments cite jurisdiction, version/year, exact code, and reference. Do not infer or invent.
10. **Verification:** Build passes and interactions have been manually tested.

## Curriculum sources
For Oklahoma, begin at https://oklahoma.gov/education/services/standards-learning/oklahoma-academic-standards.html . Verify when each version applies in the school and with the teacher before attaching codes. Don't treat NGSS as automatically interchangeable with Oklahoma's adopted standards.
