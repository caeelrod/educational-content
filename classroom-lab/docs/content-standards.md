# Content quality rubric

An agent should validate each artifact against these criteria before requesting review:

1. **Instructional objective:** One observable outcome appropriate to the section's level.
2. **Conceptual accuracy:** Sources are authoritative; uncertainty and model limitations are explicit.
3. **Misconceptions:** At least two likely misconceptions identified and addressed.
4. **Active learning:** A student makes predictions, manipulates variables, explains an outcome, or solves a challenge.
5. **Feedback:** More than an arbitrary success/failure indicator; explanations help understanding.
6. **Accessibility:** Keyboard, touch, high contrast, accessible names, plain language at the section's reading level, reduced motion when applicable.
7. **Teacher utility:** Facilitation notes for whoever teaches the lesson, duration, differentiated prompts, answer guidance.
8. **Safety:** No dangerous experiment instructions for the audience's age and setting, and no collection of learner information.
9. **Standards:** Alignments cite the source the section guide names (for example jurisdiction, version/year, exact code, and reference). Do not infer or invent.
10. **Verification:** Build passes and interactions have been manually tested.

## Section-specific expectations
Each section guide in `docs/sections/` sets its reading level, review authority, and curriculum sources, and can add criteria to this rubric. For example, the Grade School guide holds the Oklahoma standards guidance.
