# Grade School section

Id: `grade-school` · Lessons: `src/lessons/grade-school/` · Curriculum: `curriculum/grade-school/`

## Audience and level
Elementary students. The section was first built for a fifth-grade classroom, and every current lesson targets Grade 5. Some lessons stretch toward middle-school topics (for example Inside a Human Cell); their briefs say so and ask the teacher to confirm placement.

## Language level
Grade-appropriate explanations in simple language. Introduce technical vocabulary only when the lesson needs it, define it in place, and keep optional deeper terms out of the main path.

## Review authority
The classroom teacher. A lesson stays `demo` until the teacher approves its accuracy, age appropriateness, and usage context and the approval is recorded in its `teacher-guide.md` with reviewer and date.

## Curriculum sources
- Oklahoma Academic Standards: https://oklahoma.gov/education/services/standards-learning/oklahoma-academic-standards.html
- Verify when each version applies in the school and with the teacher before attaching codes. Don't treat NGSS as automatically interchangeable with Oklahoma's adopted standards.
- Record the confirmed pacing guide in `curriculum/grade-school/scope-and-sequence.md` and crosswalks in `curriculum/grade-school/standards/`.

## Setting and devices
Whole-class use on a projector or interactive whiteboard, and individual work on school Chromebooks and tablets. Many school networks filter sites or block external assets, so lessons should not depend on them.

## Extra safety and privacy rules
Learners are children. On top of the shared policies:
- Hands-on experiment suggestions must suit the classroom age, materials, and supervision available.
- Never collect anything that could identify a student, even optionally.
